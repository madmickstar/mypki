What this CA does not do (yet)

  • Revocation reaches only clients that go looking for it. From 0.7.0 this host signs a CRL and answers OCSP (/crl), and revocations are recorded on Issued with a Status column. What has not changed is the client side: certificates issued before 0.7.0 carry no pointer to either, and openssl does not fetch one even when it is baked in. For those certificates a revoke still means "this host says no" rather than "no client will accept it", so treat long validity periods as a decision you have to live with.
  • No accounts, no logins. Anyone who can reach this port can ask it to sign. Binding and TLS are the access control (see Settings for where this process is listening).
  • No delete. A record is evidence of what this CA signed; revoking stamps it, deleting would make a valid certificate look never-issued.
  • No API. There is no JSON endpoint, no /docs, and no OpenAPI document — the knobs that would enable them are hard-coded off in the app factory.
